App Description

Discover how MR Hand Exercise can transform rehabilitation therapy

The MR Hand Exercise app is designed for Meta Quest VR headsets and accessories, specifically created for individuals with limited hand mobility who require daily hand-exercise therapy.

Our innovative system helps users strengthen hand muscles with guided exercises, incorporating motivational music, interactive avatars, and real-time feedback systems. Using MR + pass-through technology to track hand movements, the app provides a comprehensive rehabilitation experience.

The first prototype features a 3D avatar that guides users through hand-grip routines, making therapy engaging and effective.

Exercise Stages

Progressive hand-grip exercises designed for gradual strength improvement

1

0 to 0.5mm Pressure Grip

Beginner level for light pressure control and initial hand mobility exercises.

  • Light pressure control
  • 10 counts of hand squeezes
  • Focus on finger extension
  • Guided breathing exercises
2

0 to 1mm Pressure Grip

Intermediate level for moderate pressure and improved hand coordination.

  • Moderate pressure control
  • Another set of 10 counts
  • Increased resistance
  • Coordination exercises
3

0 to 1.5mm Full Grip

Advanced level for full strength grip and complete hand mobility restoration.

  • Full strength grip
  • 10 counts with maximum pressure
  • Complete range of motion
  • Endurance building

Installation Guide

Follow these simple steps to install the APK on your Meta Quest device

1

Download the APK

Click the download button on this website to get the MR Hand Exercise APK file on your computer.

2

Connect Meta Quest to PC

Use a USB-C cable to connect your Meta Quest VR headset to your computer. Ensure the headset is turned on.

3

Enable Developer Mode

In the Meta Quest mobile app, go to Settings → Developer Mode and enable it. You may need to create a developer organization first.

4

Install SideQuest (Optional)

Download and install SideQuest on your computer for easier APK installation. This is recommended for beginners.

5

Sideload the APK

Use SideQuest or ADB commands to sideload the APK file to your Meta Quest device.

6

Open the App in VR

Put on your headset, go to Unknown Sources in your library, and launch the MR Hand Exercise app.
